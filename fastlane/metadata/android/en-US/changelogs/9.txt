:sparkles: New features

- Backup and restore: export and import your contacts, messages and call logs, with editable backup file names!
- Search inside a conversation, with highlighted matches and next/previous navigation!
- Search conversations by message content, with an option to turn it on or off and match counts shown!
- Favorite contacts now appear as cards in a horizontal row, and pinned contacts get their own cards!
- Unread message counts now show on conversation badges, including pinned conversations!
- Conversation details now show a media viewer, blocked email and participant contact information!
- Crash reports are saved, and the next time you open the app you can review or email them!
- Added an "Add to contact" option in call logs!
- Call logs show which SIM was used on multi-SIM phones!
- You can schedule messages to multiple recipients!
- Quick replies can now be sent to several recipients at once!
- The new Material 3 Expressive redesign includes a new font, moved search bars and smoother transitions!

:wrench: Improvements

- Contacts, call logs and conversations now load in pages, so long lists open much faster and scroll more smoothly!
- Opening individual conversations is much faster!
- Large image attachments are automatically compressed to fit carrier MMS limits!
- Phone numbers are now parsed using your SIM's home region, which gives better results when you travel!
- MMS now uses your default messaging SIM, and MMS notifications are sent!
- The SMS segment count is now calculated accurately!
- SMS delivery reports and sent/failed status are tracked more reliably!
- Improved contact editing, with better photo and poster editing, company details saved and block/unblock dialogs!
- Better accessibility labels across contacts, messages and attachments!
- Pinned and avatar images are sharper, and several animations feel less clunky!
- Improved call handling and DTMF keypad tones!
- Scheduling is disabled when attachments are present, which avoids failed sends!

:bug: Bug fixes & other

- Fixed MMS attachments not working and images scaling incorrectly in MMS!
- Fixed some incoming SMS being dropped, and multipart SMS now arrive as one message!
- Incoming SMS now keep the SIM they were received on!
- Fixed MMS delivery status being shown wrongly!
- Fixed notification replies failing when there was no input or the notification was gone!
- Fixed conversations being marked as read while not on screen!
- Fixed colliding SMS delivery intents on long multipart messages!
- Fixed multiple emojis overlapping!
- Fixed possible crashes with call notifications and with malformed MMS content!
- The app now goes back from a conversation after all its messages are deleted!
- Fixed insets on the call screen and error feedback when editing a contact!

Thanks for using Cinnamon :heart:!!!
